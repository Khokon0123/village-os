VillageOS ClimatePack

VillageOS judges a site's water by the rain it can catch and its energy by a flat 2,500 kWh per resident. For the hot, dry sites in the India and UAE pilots, both verdicts come out wrong. This prototype adds the water those places actually use and the electricity they actually need, and reruns VillageOS's own worked example in Dubai and Jaipur.

Water beyond rain

Recycled wastewater for irrigation, groundwater up to a sustainable yield, and desalination where a site reaches the sea, with the energy each one takes.

Energy for heat

Cooling electricity from the site's cooling degree days, fetched from NASA POWER, the same provider VillageOS's climate registration calls.

Both sites use the Willow Bend programme from VillageOS's LAND_INTAKE guide: 24 hectares, 320 residents, the same six programme shares. Only the climate changes.

How it fits VillageOS

exact

Reproduces the Willow Bend worked example before changing anything: 49,728 m³ harvest, 8,672 m³ irrigation shortfall, 91% of consumption.

20 tests

Pass, including the pipeline envelope, the manifest, and failing by port name on a missing or withheld input.

drop-in

A Python node on vos.Service.Python.Shared, the same contract as the Echo template, beside EnergyBalance and WaterReserve.

It also includes a draft Tributary registration that fetches cooling degree days onto a Site, written in the shape of climate-averages. It has not yet been run through the seed validator.

Limits, and what a summer would settle

  1. Annual totals hide the seasons. Jaipur's rain falls in a few monsoon months, so storage needs the daily rain VillageOS already fetches.
  2. Cooling uses a simple degree-day model. Humidity, building type and the study's wet-bulb figures should refine it.
  3. Air-conditioner efficiency, floor area per person, wastewater return and pump lift have no regional source yet. Pinning them for India and the UAE, with citations, is the core research.
  4. Today it is a pipeline node. Folded into the platform, its formulas would become derived properties on the shared study, judged by ranges like every other balance.
